===================================

Jan 9, 2023






Active trading has come under fire as of late with the Robinhood and GME sagas. With the usual suspects for casting blame being High Frequency Trading and Payment for Order Flow.

But is it fair to label PFOF as being bad for the retail trader? As we see today, the exact opposite is actually true.
